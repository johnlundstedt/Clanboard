Questions families ask

Do I need to buy special hardware?

No. Clanboard runs in a web browser, so you can use any modern phone, tablet or computer. A second-hand tablet works well as a wall display.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every new clan gets 30 days free with full access to every feature. No credit card is required to start.

Does Clanboard work with Google Calendar?

Yes. Connect one or more Google Calendars and their events appear on your family calendar. Google Calendar events are shown read-only for now.

Do I need to download an app?

No app store needed. Open clanboard.app in your browser and add it to your home screen. It opens full screen, just like an app.

How many family members can I add?

Your subscription covers your whole household on all of your devices.

Can kids use it?

Yes. Kids can see their chores for the day and check them off themselves. Little ones who can’t read yet can tap a chore to hear it read aloud.

How much does Clanboard cost?

After your 30-day free trial, Clanboard is $5 per month or $50 per year for your whole family.
